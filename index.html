📖 How to Play

  • 🎯
    Predict every match — go to Fixtures, pick Team 1, Draw, or Team 2 for each match. Predictions lock 15 minutes before kick-off and cannot be changed after that.
  • ⭐
    Tournament Picks — before the first group match kicks off, rank your top 4 teams to win the World Cup. These lock at the same time as the first match and cannot be changed.
  • 🏅
    Points per correct prediction:
    • Group Stage — 2 pts
    • Round of 32 — 3 pts
    • Round of 16 — 4 pts
    • Quarter-finals — 5 pts
    • Semi-finals / Third Place — 6 pts
    • Final — 8 pts
  • 🎁
    Tournament Pick bonuses (awarded after the tournament ends):
    • 1st place correct — +10 pts
    • 2nd place correct — +7 pts
    • 3rd place correct — +5 pts
    • 4th place correct — +3 pts
  • 👥
    Leagues — create or join a private league with a code to compete on a separate leaderboard against friends.
